AudioTrim Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

AudioTrim does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information. Your audio stays on your device.

What AudioTrim does with your audio

When you import audio, open an audio file with AudioTrim, or share audio to AudioTrim from another app, the file is copied into AudioTrim's private storage on your iPhone so you can play, trim, and organize it. Clips, folders, and trimmed exports are saved only on your device.

AudioTrim has no network access. It doesn't upload your audio or any information about it to us or anyone else.

Data we collect

None. AudioTrim has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting software, and no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

  • Your imported audio files and the clips you create.
  • Your folder names and clip organization.
  • A setting that records whether you have finished the introduction screens.

This data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, according to your own backup settings. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Sharing and exporting

When you choose to share or export a clip, AudioTrim hands the file to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you and the app or service you pick.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual clips and folders inside AudioTrim. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

AudioTrim doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/yonnytan/audiotrim-privacy/issues.
